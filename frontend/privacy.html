Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

CafeLoop is a cafe-retention service for cafe owners ("owners"). This policy explains what data we store and why.

Owner accounts

We store the owner's name, cafe name, address, city, phone numbers, email and a securely hashed password (never the plain password). Sign-in with Google is optional; if used, we receive the name and email from Google.

Customer data

When a customer orders through a cafe's public page, the cafe receives their name, phone number and order history. CafeLoop stores this so the cafe's stamp card, rewards, favourite items and Come Back list work. Customers never create accounts with CafeLoop, and we never sell customer data. Each cafe can only see its own customers.

Payments

Owner subscriptions are collected directly by the CafeLoop team (UPI). We do not store card or UPI credentials. Customer payments happen at the cafe counter, outside CafeLoop.

Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes; sign-in codes are stored hashed and expire quickly; sessions are signed cookies; accounts are isolated per cafe.

Your choices

Owners can request export or deletion of their account data by contacting the CafeLoop team. Deleting an account removes its menu, customers and orders.
